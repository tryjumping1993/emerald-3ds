# Thor launcher artwork

The launcher uses [rayquaza.png](app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/rayquaza.png),
generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-19 at the maintainer's
request. It is new fan artwork depicting Rayquaza; Pokémon and character
rights belong to their respective owners. It is not extracted game art.
The smaller launcher PNGs are resized copies, and the adaptive icon adds
padding for Android's launcher masks.

Final generation prompt:

> A single square Android launcher icon for an Emerald game fan project,
> featuring Rayquaza, the emerald-green serpentine dragon Pokémon, curled
> in a compact dynamic pose. Recognizable angular dragon head, yellow ring
> markings, yellow eyes and red mouth. Clean bold cel-shaded illustration,
> deep forest-green background, luminous emerald and restrained gold,
> strong readable silhouette at small size. No text or letters, no
> watermark, no extra characters. Center the dragon inside the middle
> two-thirds of the image so Android's circular and rounded-square masks
> do not cut off its face. Flat finished icon artwork, not a device mockup.
